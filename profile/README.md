## Who we are

We are the **IT Department of Jaime Mascaró S.A.**, a shoemaking company founded in 1918 in Menorca, with brands including [Mascaró](https://www.mascaro.com) and [Pretty Ballerinas](https://www.prettyballerinas.com).

Our team combines technical support, systems administration, and in-house software development to support the entire company's operations: from the factory in Ferreries to our own stores and digital channels around the world.

---

## What we do

We build tools and automations that **eliminate manual work and streamline internal workflows** across the company.

```
Systems integration   ──►  SAP Business One · Shopify · Mirakl · ECI
Automation             ──►  Reports, catalogs, data synchronization
Support & admin        ──►  Infrastructure, users, networks
```

Some examples of what we build:

- 📊 **Automated reports** — weekly sales KPIs generated and sent without manual intervention
- 🔗 **SAP ↔ Marketplace connectors** — catalog and stock synchronization between SAP B1 and platforms like Mirakl or ECI
- 🖨️ **Production tools** — generation of catalog PDFs, thermal labels, and seasonal documents
- 🤖 **MCP servers** — integrating AI models with internal systems via the MCP protocol
- 🏢 **B2B portal** — online ordering platform for wholesale customers
- 🔌 **Internal API** — integration layer between SAP B1, Shopify, and the rest of the company's systems
- ⚙️ **Cutting optimization engine (C++)** — leather usage calculation for production
